About Talk House

Talk House is a digital communication platform built to connect people through meaningful, paid conversations. We create safe, simple ways for registered hosts to earn by offering companionship, advice, and conversation to curious users around the world. Beyond earnings, Talk House helps people meet friends and partners, discover new perspectives, and build confidence through real human connection.

Our mission

To empower everyday people with flexible earning opportunities through respectful, moderated conversations. Human connection has value — Talk House makes it possible to be paid for the time and care you give.

How it works

Register, pay a one-time fee, set your availability, and connect with people. The platform handles matching so you can focus on the conversation.

Privacy & safety

Safety and privacy are core priorities. Moderation tools, community guidelines, and secure payments protect every user. Hosts control what they share and can block abusive users at any time.
